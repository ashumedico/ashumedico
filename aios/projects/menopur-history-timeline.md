## THE NARRATIVE

**The problem hMG was born to solve.** For most of medical history there was no way to stimulate the
ovaries. The breakthrough came from an unlikely place — the urine of postmenopausal women, which is
rich in pituitary gonadotropins (FSH and LH) the body excretes after menopause. Human menopausal
gonadotropin (hMG, INN **menotropin**) was first extracted from postmenopausal urine around 1949–1950,
and the first commercial preparation (Pergonal, Serono) emerged from that work. [sourced — PMC6616070; **[GAP]** exact year 1949 vs 1950 differs by source]

**1961 — the door opens.** Bruno Lunenfeld's group delivered the first child conceived after hMG-based
ovarian stimulation in 1961 — the event that "opened the path to controlled ovarian stimulation," the
foundation of all modern IVF. [VERIFIED — Lunenfeld 2012, *Reprod Med Biol*]

**The safety reckoning of the late 1980s.** A competing product, human *pituitary* gonadotropin (HPG),
gave good results but was withdrawn in the late 1980s after being linked to Creutzfeldt–Jakob disease
(CJD). That left urinary hMG as the dominant gonadotropin — but early hMG carried a high percentage of
unknown urinary proteins, making quality control almost impossible. [VERIFIED — Lunenfeld 2012]

**The purification race → Menopur.** The answer to the "dirty urine protein" problem was purification.
Ferring developed **highly purified hMG (HP-hMG) = Menopur**, standardised to **75 IU FSH + 75 IU LH
activity per vial**, where the LH activity is supplied mainly by **hCG** (≈10 IU hCG per 75 IU FSH) rather
than native LH. This 1:1 FSH:LH-activity design is Menopur's defining signature versus pure recombinant
FSH. [sourced — PMC8594316; US FDA label/DailyMed]

**The evidence era (2006–2020).** Once purified and standardised, Menopur was tested head-to-head against
recombinant FSH (rFSH) in a series of large RCTs and meta-analyses — MERiT (2006), Coomarasamy
meta-analysis (2008), the Cochrane review (2011), MEGASET (2012), and MEGASET-HR (2020). The arc of that
evidence: Menopur is **at least as effective** as rFSH for live birth, with signals of **better embryo
quality** and, in high responders, **meaningfully less OHSS and pregnancy loss**. (Details below.)

**The convenience era (2018–2021+).** The latest chapter is delivery, not molecule: Menopur moved from a
powder-for-reconstitution to a **stable liquid in a pre-filled multi-dose pen** with a fine 31G needle,
shown bioequivalent to the old powder. [VERIFIED — PMC8594316]

**Where it stands today.** Modern guidelines and the Cochrane evidence treat rFSH and HP-hMG as broadly
equivalent for live birth, with product choice driven by patient profile, OHSS risk, convenience and
cost — Menopur's distinct edge being its LH activity and a favourable OHSS profile in high responders.

## THE PIVOTAL TRIALS (detail)

### MERiT — 2006 · Andersen et al., *Human Reproduction* 21(12):3217
- **Design:** Randomized, assessor-blind, multinational RCT.
- **Population:** 731 women undergoing IVF (HP-hMG n=363; rFSH n=368), **long GnRH agonist** protocol.
- **Comparator:** Recombinant FSH (Gonal-F). **Primary endpoint:** ongoing pregnancy rate.
- **Result:** 27% (HP-hMG) vs 22% (rFSH); **OR 1.25 (95% CI 0.89–1.75)** — **non-inferiority** established;
  superiority not concluded. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### Ziebe (embryo quality, MERiT sub-analysis) — 2007 · *Hum Reprod* 22(9):2404
- Top-quality embryos per oocyte higher with HP-hMG: **11.3% vs 9.0% (P=0.044)** by local embryologist;
  **not significant** on central panel (9.5% vs 8.0%).
- Among top-quality embryos: live birth / ongoing pregnancy **48% vs 32% (P=0.038)** — signal of better
  implantation capacity. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### Coomarasamy meta-analysis — 2008 · *Hum Reprod* 23(2):310 (RBMO)
- 7 RCTs, **2159 women**, hMG vs rFSH, long agonist.
- **Live birth RR 1.18 (95% CI 1.02–1.38, P=0.03)** — ~**4% absolute** increase favouring hMG. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### Cochrane review — 2011 · van Wely et al., CD005354.pub2
- **42 RCTs, 9606 couples**, rFSH vs urinary gonadotrophins (incl. HP-hMG).
- **No significant live-birth difference: OR 0.97 (95% CI 0.87–1.08)** (28 trials, 7339 couples).
- Conclusion: choose by **availability, convenience, cost**. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### MEGASET — 2012 · Devroey et al., *Fertility & Sterility*
- Randomized, open-label, assessor-blind, **749 women**, 25 centres, 7 countries, **GnRH antagonist**,
  compulsory **single-blastocyst** transfer.
- **Ongoing pregnancy 30% vs 27% (PP)** — non-inferior. **Cumulative live birth (incl. frozen ≤1 yr)
  40% vs 38%.** _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### MEGASET-HR — 2020 · Witz et al., *Fertility & Sterility*
- Randomized, open-label, assessor-blinded, **620 predicted high responders (AMH ≥5 ng/mL)**, GnRH
  antagonist ART/ICSI.
- **Ongoing pregnancy (fresh) 35.5% vs 30.7%** — non-inferior.
- **OHSS significantly lower: 9.7% vs 21.4%.** Early pregnancy loss **14.5% vs 25.5%.**
- Cumulative live birth similar (**50.6% vs 51.5%**) despite 43 more transfers in the rFSH arm →
  optimised risk/benefit in high responders. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

### Bordewijk meta-analysis — 2019 · *Hum Reprod Open* 2019;hoz008
- 28 RCTs, **7553 women**; total gonadotrophin dose differed only **marginally (MD −37 IU)** — near-equivalent
  dosing to reach outcomes. _[sourced, not cross-verified]_

## COMPOSITION & MECHANISM
**Menotropin (Menopur, HP-hMG)** = highly purified urinary preparation, **75 IU FSH + 75 IU LH activity per
vial (1:1)**. The LH activity is supplied **mainly by hCG** (≈10 IU hCG per 75 IU FSH) plus low native LH.
FSH drives follicular recruitment/growth; the hCG-driven LH activity supports theca-cell androgen
production and steroidogenesis — the mechanistic basis for interest in LH activity in selected patients.
[sourced — PMC8594316; FDA label] · **[GAP]** the precise hCG-per-FSH ratio could not be cross-verified this run.
