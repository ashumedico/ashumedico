# 60-Day Body Transformation — Daily Diet Plan
_Source: @9.ao.kokani | Started: 2026-06-24_

---

## DAILY SCHEDULE

| Time | Meal | What to eat |
|---|---|---|
| **6:00 AM** | Wake-up | 500ml water + 5 soaked almonds (soak overnight) |
| **8:00 AM** | Breakfast | 6 eggs (2 whole + 4 whites) + 80-100g oats/poha + 1 banana |
| **11:00 AM** | Mid-morning | 1 apple + 50g roasted chana (bhajlele harbhare) |
| **1:00 PM** | Lunch | 150-200g chicken breast (or 150g paneer/soya chunks) + 2 chapati + salad + 1 bowl dal |
| **4:30 PM** | Pre-workout | 1 banana + black coffee (NO sugar) — 45 min before workout |
| **6:00 PM** | Post-workout | 4 egg whites (or whey protein) + 1 banana |
| **8:30 PM** | Dinner | 150-200g chicken/fish/paneer + green vegetables + salad |
| **10:00 PM** | Before bed | 1 glass low-fat milk |

---

## DAILY TARGETS
- Protein: **120-140g/day**
- Training: 45-60 min strength + 20-30 min cardio
- Sleep: 7-8 hours

## AVOID (non-negotiable)
- Sugar
- Bakery items
- Cold drinks
- Fried food (talalele padarth)
