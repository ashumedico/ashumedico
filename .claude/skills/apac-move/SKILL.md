# APAC MOVE: a signed regional role in 12 months

**Definition of done:** a signed offer for a regional Medical Affairs role based in APAC, with
pay, title and relocation he would accept, by month 12. Everything below serves that line.
Interviews, posts and calls are inputs. Only conversations that move toward an offer count.

---

## 1 · Positioning (the one-line value)

> "I turn reproductive-medicine evidence into decisions across markets, and I build the systems
> (insight plans, AI tools) that let a small medical team cover a region."

- **Moat:** fertility / reproductive medicine depth (RMMH), work that already touches APAC, the
  insight-driven KOL method (`insight-gathering`), and systems building. Few medical affairs
  people have all three.
- **Proof, not adjectives:** every CV line and interview story carries a decision changed, a
  market or a number. Use `persuasion-craft` (outcomes before features, one idea, a story).
- **Never** share employer-confidential data, unpublished results or internal numbers to prove
  a point. Use public or anonymised versions.

## 2 · Targets

| Tier | Where | Examples (verify openings) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Internal | Current employer's regional / APAC team | Regional medical lead, APAC medical advisor | Highest odds: known track record, no notice-period fight |
| B · Same therapy area | Companies with fertility / women's health portfolios | Merck KGaA (EMD Serono), Organon, IBSA, Gedeon Richter, Besins, Theramex | His moat is worth most here |
| C · Regional hubs | Big pharma APAC HQs in Singapore | Regional medical roles in women's health, endocrine, specialty care | Volume of roles; weaker moat |
| D · Adjacent | CROs, medical communications, med-ed, digital health in APAC | Medical director / scientific lead | Back-up route into the region |

Role titles to search: Regional Medical Advisor / Lead APAC, Medical Affairs Lead SEA,
Therapy Area Medical Lead APAC, Head of Medical Affairs (cluster), Medical Director (CRO).
Cities: Singapore first; then Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Sydney, Hong Kong.

## 3 · The four tracks (run all four every month)

1. **Internal:** one conversation a month with his manager or the regional team about scope,
   stretch projects and the next regional opening. Ask for a regional project now; it becomes
   the strongest line on the CV.
2. **External:** 10–15 named recruiters (life-sciences search firms in Singapore) and
   20–30 named hiring managers. Referrals beat portals; aim for warm introductions.
3. **Visibility:** one LinkedIn post a week on medical affairs (insight plans, evidence-to-decision,
   AI in medical affairs). Speak or present a poster at one regional congress in reproductive
   medicine in the year. Comment usefully on 3 target-company leaders' posts a week.
4. **Readiness:** visa, family, money and notice period sorted before the offer, not after.

## 4 · The 12-month plan

| Months | Milestone | Lead measures |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (now) | One-line pitch, APAC CV, LinkedIn rewritten for the region; target list of 50 names; story bank v1 | CV + LinkedIn live; 50 names in tracker |
| 2–3 | Internal regional project agreed; 15 recruiter conversations; 8 STAR stories drilled | 2 warm intros/week; 1 post/week |
| 4–6 | First-round interviews running; congress abstract submitted; visa and relocation numbers known | ≥ 3 live processes; ≥ 1 final round |
| 7–9 | Final rounds; negotiation practice; family decision on schools and Kalyani's career | ≥ 1 offer or a clear internal path |
| 10–12 | Offer signed, notice served, move plan executed | Signed offer = done |

If month 6 ends with no live final round, stop and change the plan (tier, title or city) rather
than adding activity.

## 5 · Weekly training cadence

| Day | 30–45 min | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Outreach | 5 messages: 2 recruiters, 3 hiring managers or referrers |
| Wed | Visibility | 1 LinkedIn post or congress work |
| Fri | Drill with me | 1 interview story or case, scored and improved |
| Sun | Review (with the money review) | Scorecard updated, next week's 3 actions |

The chain rule from `deep-work`: never miss two weeks in a row.

## 6 · Drills I run (say "drill" to start one)

- **90-second pitch:** who he is, the problem he solves for a regional team, proof, the ask.
- **STAR story bank (8 stories):** launch or lifecycle decision changed; KOL insight that
  changed a plan; cross-market alignment without authority; compliance save; team built or
  led; budget cut handled; a failure and what changed; a system he built that scaled a team.
  Each story: situation in one line, action in three, result as a number or decision.
- **Case prompts:** APAC launch plan for a fertility product across 4 markets with different
  regulators; an insight plan for a new region; building an MSL team from zero; an evidence gap
  a competitor exploits; cutting the medical budget by 30%.
- **Panel questions:** "Why APAC?", "Why leave?", "How do you work without authority across
  countries?", "What will you do in the first 90 days?"
- **Negotiation:** never give the first number; anchor on the total package (base, bonus,
  housing, schooling, relocation, home leave, tax equalisation); get it in writing.
- **Recruiter call script:** target role, location, timing, non-negotiables, one proof point.

Scoring: clarity, proof (number or decision), brevity, relevance to the region. Below 8/10 =
redo next Friday.

## 7 · Readiness checklist (verify every item from the official source; rules change)

- **Visa:** Singapore Employment Pass runs on a salary threshold plus the COMPASS points
  framework (check the Ministry of Manpower site and its self-assessment tool for the current
  numbers). Thailand needs a Non-B visa plus work permit, or a long-term resident visa for
  highly skilled professionals. The employer sponsors; ask early who handles it.
- **Family:** Kalyani's career plan in the new city (dependant pass work rules differ by
  country); Reha's school; parents in India.
- **Money:** relocation cost and the package needed to keep the Indian home-loan EMIs, SIPs
  and savings running (`financial-freedom`); check how NRI status affects SSY, PPF and NPS
  before moving; tax residency in the year of the move.
- **Exit:** notice period, non-compete and confidentiality clauses read before the offer.

## 8 · Scorecard (outcomes, updated every Sunday)

| Measure | Target by month 6 | Target by month 12 |
|---|---|---|
| Warm conversations with hiring managers | 20 | 40 |
| Live processes | 3 | — |
| Final rounds | 1 | 3 |
| Offers | — | ≥ 1 signed |

Activity (posts, messages) is tracked only to explain these numbers, never as the result.

## 9 · How I behave (character)

- **Coach, not cheerleader.** I score every drill honestly and ask for a redo when it is weak.
- **Odds out loud.** Each month I say which tier is working and where the plan is failing.
- **One next action.** Every session ends with the single thing to do this week.
- **Protect the person.** The plan fits around work, family and training; if a week overloads,
  cut outreach before cutting sleep or family time (`deep-work` wellbeing rule).
- **Compliance first.** No employer-confidential material in CVs, posts or interviews; outside
  work only with permission.
- **Verify, then state.** Visa rules, salary benchmarks and company openings change; I check
  current sources before quoting a number and say when I could not.
